# Week 6 architecture review preparation

## Driver-to-container review map

This map prepares the focused review carried from the [Week 5 action points](../week-05/meeting-report.md#action-points).
It maps the current design to its quality scenarios; it records neither a new architectural decision nor Customer acceptance.

| Driver and measurable scenario | Containers and existing decision | Evidence to bring to the Customer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [QR-01](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md): shared-only access and key confidentiality | `frontend` keeps a personal key and calls the provider; `backend` authorizes shared-deck access; `database` stores explicit shares, per [ADR-002](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-002.md) and [ADR-004](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-004.md). | [Account/isolation tests](../../backend/tests/test_accounts.py) and [mocked browser-key tests](../../frontend/tests/client.test.mjs), plus the outstanding separate-account real-provider run required by the scenario. |
| [QR-03](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-03.md), [QR-04](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-04.md), [QR-05](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-05.md), and [QR-09](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-09.md): suitable cards, bounded generation, recoverable failures, and batch warnings | `backend` builds and checks generation requests; `frontend` handles browser-key requests and feedback; `database` retains committed cards and marks, per [ADR-002](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-002.md) and [ADR-003](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-003.md). | [Generation regressions](../../backend/tests/test_week4_changes.py), the current retry/warning calculation, and the missing real-model language-quality, timing, cost, and failure results; a fake-provider pass does not establish language quality. |
| [QR-07](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-07.md): committed learning data survives restart | `database` persists application data; `backend` applies migrations and reconnects, within the split in [ADR-001](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-001.md). | The [restart procedure](../../scripts/check-restart.sh) and its recorded [testing-plan evidence](../../docs/project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan), identifying the original environment and commit rather than implying a Week 6 rerun. |
| [QR-08](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-08.md): repeatable Linux installation and core workflow | `frontend`, `backend`, and `database` are deployed together, per [ADR-001](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-001.md). | [Setup prerequisites](../../docs/setup.md#prerequisites), [deployment steps](../../docs/deployment.md), and a clean Linux/deployment run with its commit; the runbook alone is not execution evidence. |
| [FR-010](../../docs/functional-requirements/fr/FR-010.md): sentence pronunciation and playback behavior | `frontend` owns the current [sound component](../../frontend/src/components/Say.tsx) and user controls; browser speech is the present implementation. | The [browser/server/API comparison](pronunciation-options.md), language and failure checks, and the Customer's remaining source/storage/reuse choice; this map selects no new service or container. |
| [QR-06](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-06.md): usable learner and teacher workflows on a phone | `frontend` renders the reader, study session, and teacher editor; `backend` enforces their access and data rules. | The requirement's phone-width procedure with long-content cases, plus outside-user observations; compilation and source inspection do not establish layout or comprehension results. |
| [QR-10](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-10.md) and [SLI-01](../../docs/observability.md#sli-01): diagnosable failed requests | `backend` exports request logs and duration histograms to external OpenObserve, per [ADR-007](../../docs/architecture/adr/ADR-007.md). | The staging failed request's log point, failure labels, P95 result, environment/commit, and dashboard screenshot; these remain distinct from product CI. |
