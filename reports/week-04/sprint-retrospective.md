The retrospective of [SPRINT-04](../../docs/project-plan.md#sprint-04), written at the sprint's close after its Actual Velocity was set.

| What went well |
| --- |
| Real LLM generation and rewriting ran in front of the Customer, and US-02 and US-03 closed under [DEC-082](../../docs/decisions.md#dec-082). |
| The quality requirements, functional requirements, architecture, ADRs, glossary, and `AGENTS.md` each merged through a pull request that closed its task issue ([#118](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/pull/118), [#126](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/pull/126), [#125](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/pull/125)). |

## What Did Not

| Problem | Root cause |
| --- | --- |
| The sprint had no Planned Velocity before the work started, and its stories entered the milestone only on 2026-10-24, the review day. | The planning task ([#116](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/116)) had no owner, and no planning session was held at the start of the sprint. |
| All eight sprint pull requests merged on 2026-10-25, after the sprint's due date, and one member authored seven of them. | The parts were not split into owned tasks with mid-week dates, so the work queued behind one person and the final day. |
| The Sprint Review did not take a verdict on each `AC-nn`, and US-08 left without agreed criteria for its rework. | The script asked for one verdict per story rather than per criterion, and the verdicts came last, in about three minutes at the end of a 94-minute meeting. |
