## Summary

- The production demonstration showed card generation through DeepSeek and exposed missing first-use guidance, confusing repeated-word highlighting, unclear card/deck actions, and a generated card with two sentences.
- The Customer required German learners in the usability sample, welcomed participants without Anki experience, and rejected 60% completion as satisfactory in favor of an expectation closer to 80-90%; the exact QR-11 bar still needs agreement.
- The minimum usable product must include studying and returning for a later scheduled review, with usable pronunciation; regeneration should preserve progress and offer a deliberate reset, and deck study needs consistent navigation, statistics, ordering, and limits.
- The Customer confirmed an 80% acceptable-card quality target and specified Linux deployment within 10 GB RAM, ordinary responses within one second, an LLM wait of no more than one minute, and data preservation through a VM restart; the meeting reported no new measurements of these targets.
- The Customer acknowledged the private evidence arrangement, but invitation acceptance, the full staging/instrumentation and survey/QR review, and the exact Week 8 session/results arrangements remain to be confirmed; the [UT-01 record](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), [surveys](../../docs/usability-tests/ut-01/surveys.md), and [QR-11](../../docs/quality-requirements/qr/QR-11.md) require the corresponding design rework before counted sessions.
