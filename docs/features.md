# Features

The capabilities of the Sentence Cards Generator, in the order a user meets them.
The words this product uses in its own sense are in the [glossary](glossary.md).

## Bring your own reading material

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can save a [text](glossary.md#text) they want to learn from.

- **Stories:** [US-01](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/30)
- **Behavior:** [FR-001](functional-requirements/fr/FR-001.md)

## Choose words to learn in context

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can choose which words to learn from a [text](glossary.md#text) in the meanings they encounter there.

- **Stories:** [US-02](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/31)
- **Behavior:** [FR-002](functional-requirements/fr/FR-002.md)

## Turn chosen words into sentence cards

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can turn a [marked word](glossary.md#marked-word) into a [card](glossary.md#card) with a new sentence to study in a [deck](glossary.md#deck).

- **Stories:** [US-03](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/32), [US-05](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/38), [US-06](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/39)
- **Behavior:** [FR-003](functional-requirements/fr/FR-003.md), [FR-004](functional-requirements/fr/FR-004.md), [FR-005](functional-requirements/fr/FR-005.md), [FR-012](functional-requirements/fr/FR-012.md)
- **Decisions:** [ADR-002](architecture/adr/ADR-002.md), [ADR-003](architecture/adr/ADR-003.md)

## Improve a card before studying it

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can correct or [rewrite](glossary.md#rewrite) a [card](glossary.md#card) to make it useful to study.

- **Stories:** [US-04](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/37)
- **Behavior:** [FR-006](functional-requirements/fr/FR-006.md)

## Study the decks you choose

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can study selected [decks](glossary.md#deck) with reviews scheduled to help them remember their [cards](glossary.md#card).

- **Stories:** [US-09](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/42), [US-10](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/36)
- **Behavior:** [FR-008](functional-requirements/fr/FR-008.md), [FR-009](functional-requirements/fr/FR-009.md)
- **Decisions:** [ADR-005](architecture/adr/ADR-005.md)

## Give feedback on difficult sentences

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can flag a sentence as too hard to guide future [card](glossary.md#card) generation.

- **Stories:** [US-07](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/40)
- **Behavior:** [FR-007](functional-requirements/fr/FR-007.md)

## Listen to a sentence

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can hear a [card](glossary.md#card)'s sentence spoken in the language they are learning.

- **Stories:** [US-08](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/41)
- **Behavior:** [FR-010](functional-requirements/fr/FR-010.md)

## Get a teacher's help with a deck

A [learner](glossary.md#learner) can share a [deck](glossary.md#deck) with a [teacher](glossary.md#teacher) for feedback and corrections to its [cards](glossary.md#card).

- **Stories:** [US-11](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/35), [US-12](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/34)
- **Behavior:** [FR-011](functional-requirements/fr/FR-011.md)
- **Decisions:** [ADR-004](architecture/adr/ADR-004.md)
