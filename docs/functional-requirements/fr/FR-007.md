# FR-007: Flag A Sentence As Too Hard

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-07`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/40)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When a learner flags a card as too hard while studying, the product shall record the flag with the review.

When cards are next generated for that learner, the product shall send the learner's most recent too-hard sentences, at most five, as examples of what to make simpler.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a learner who flags the card `Der Zug hat wegen einer Signalstörung Verspätung.` as too hard, when they next generate cards, then that sentence is among the too-hard examples in the generation request.

### AC-02

Given a learner who flagged seven cards as too hard, when they generate cards, then the request carries the five most recent ones.
