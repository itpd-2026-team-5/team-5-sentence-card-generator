# FR-002: Mark Words To Learn In A Text

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-02`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/31), [`DEC-016`](../../decisions.md#dec-016), [`ASM-04`](../../assumptions.md#asm-04)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - A word is marked in one sentence, so the same word can be learned in two sentences, for two meanings; ignoring a word stays for the whole text; each mark shows its meaning in its sentence and the decks that have the word, per the Customer in the Week 4 meeting; its `DEC-nnn` is recorded with that meeting's report.

## Behavior

When a learner chooses a word in a sentence of the reader, the product shall let them mark it to learn in that sentence, mark it to ignore in the whole text, or clear its mark.

When the learner marks the same word to learn in a second sentence, the product shall keep both marks, one per sentence.

When a word is marked, the product shall keep the sentence it was marked in as the word's context.

For each word marked to learn, the product shall show the word's translation as it is used in its sentence, and the learner's decks that already have a card for the word.

When the learner generates cards for a text, the product shall count the text's words that the learner left unmarked as words the learner knows.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given the reader open on a text, when the learner marks `Gleis` to learn, `Bahnhof` to ignore, and then clears `Bahnhof`, then the text shows one word to learn and no ignored word after a reload.

### AC-02

Given `Bank` marked in `Ich sitze auf der Bank im Park.`, when the mark is saved, then its context is that sentence.

### AC-03

Given a text where the learner marked only `Gleis`, when cards are generated, then the other words of the text are in the list of known words sent with the generation request, and `Gleis` is not.

### AC-04

Given `Bank` marked to learn in `Ich sitze auf der Bank im Park.` and in `Danach bringe ich mein Geld zur Bank.`, when the text is reloaded, then it has two marks for `Bank`, and generating creates a card for each sentence.

### AC-05

Given those two marks, when their meanings have been looked up, then the park mark shows `bench` and the money mark shows `bank`, each beside the word.
