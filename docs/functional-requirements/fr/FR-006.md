# FR-006: Correct A Card

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-04`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/37), [`DEC-018`](../../decisions.md#dec-018), [`DEC-033`](../../decisions.md#dec-033), [`DEC-034`](../../decisions.md#dec-034), [`DEC-035`](../../decisions.md#dec-035)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When a learner edits a card's sentence, translation, word, or word translation, the product shall save the edit and show the edited card in study.

When the learner asks to rewrite a card, with or without instructions, the product shall replace its sentence and translations with a new generated one, keep the instructions with the card, and keep its review progress.

When the learner marks cards to rewrite later and then rewrites the marked cards of a deck, the product shall rewrite each marked card and clear its mark.

When the learner archives a card, the product shall keep it in the deck and take it out of study; when the learner deletes a card, the product shall remove it.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a card, when the learner changes its sentence and opens study, then the card shows the changed sentence.

### AC-02

Given a card with two reviews, when the learner rewrites it with the instruction `use the past tense`, then the card has a new sentence, shows that instruction, and still has two reviews.

### AC-03

Given a deck with three cards marked to rewrite, when the learner rewrites the marked cards, then the three cards have new sentences and none is still marked.

### AC-04

Given a card, when the learner archives it, then the deck's archived filter lists it and study does not show it; when they delete another card, then the deck no longer lists that card.
