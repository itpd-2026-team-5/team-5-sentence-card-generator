## Behavior

When a learner edits a card's sentence, translation, word, or word translation, the product shall save the edit and show the edited card in study.

When the learner asks to rewrite a card, with or without instructions, the product shall replace its sentence and translations with a new generated one, keep the instructions with the card, and keep its review progress.

When the learner marks cards to rewrite later and then rewrites the marked cards of a deck, the product shall rewrite each marked card and clear its mark.

When the learner archives a card, the product shall keep it in the deck and take it out of study; when the learner deletes a card, the product shall remove it.

When a teacher archives a shared card, the product shall keep the card available to its learner for inspection or permanent deletion under [FR-011](FR-011.md); sharing shall not grant the teacher permanent-deletion permission.

When the learner views deck summaries, the product shall show both the learning and translation languages for each deck.

When a deck's cards open their editor on selection and have no separate Edit button, the product shall display an instruction explaining that selecting a card opens its editor.

When the learner adds an existing card to another deck, the product shall keep the same card and its review history.

When the learner changes a card's deck memberships, the product shall keep at least one membership and shall not interpret a membership change as permanent card deletion.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a card, when the learner changes its sentence and opens study, then the card shows the changed sentence.

### AC-02

Given a card with two reviews, when the learner rewrites it with the instruction `use the past tense`, then the card has a new sentence, shows that instruction, and still has two reviews.

### AC-03

Given a deck with three cards marked to rewrite, when the learner rewrites the marked cards, then the three cards have new sentences and none is still marked.

### AC-04

Given a card, when the learner archives it, then the deck's archived filter lists it and study does not show it; when they delete another card, then the deck no longer lists that card.

### AC-05

Given a German-to-English deck and a Russian-to-German deck, when their summaries are shown, then each summary identifies both of its languages.

### AC-06

Given cards open their editor when selected and no separate Edit button is displayed, when the learner opens the deck, then an instruction explains how to open a card's editor and selecting a card opens it.

### AC-07

Given a card in decks A and B with two saved reviews, when the learner removes its membership in A, then it remains in B with the same identifier and two reviews; an attempt to remove its final membership leaves it in at least one deck and does not delete it.
