## Behavior

When a learner shares a deck with the email of a teacher who has an account, the product shall show that deck and all its cards to that teacher, and nothing else of the learner's.

When a learner asks the teacher about a card, the product shall accept the request only if the card's deck is shared, and shall list the cards asked about first on the teacher's page.

When a signed-in user opens Teaching, the product shall show the account email used for receiving deck shares and shall make Teaching available even if no deck has been shared with that user.

When the teacher reviews a shared deck on desktop, the product shall show the sentence, translation, and approval control in each table row and shall let the teacher open the card for more information.

When the teacher views shared cards on a phone, the product shall show each card's sentence and translation in its summary and shall keep review controls from overlapping long words or translations.

When a teacher opens a card's details, the product shall provide separate pronunciation controls there for the word, meaning, sentence, and translation, rather than adding them to the summary table.

When the teacher edits a shared card or its review status, the product shall keep the change pending until the teacher explicitly saves it, and the learner shall study the saved correction.

When the teacher opens a card's editor, the product shall show the generated sentence and its translation before the word and word-meaning editing fields.

When the teacher opens a card's editor, the product shall show its original source sentence above the generated sentence, keep that source sentence read-only, and highlight the selected word in it.

When the teacher corrects a card's word or its meaning and explicitly saves, the product shall update that same card and preserve its identifier, original source context, deck memberships, review history, and next due date.

When the teacher requests regeneration, the product shall accept corrective feedback separately from the learner-authored generation instructions and shall not let the teacher modify those instructions.

When regeneration controls are shown, the product shall identify the action as regenerating the card and explain the corrective-feedback and existing-instruction inputs used to generate its sentence and translation.

When regeneration returns a result, the product shall display the latest sentence and translation below the regeneration button in the same editor so the teacher can inspect and retry it; on desktop the preview and rewrite actions shall be visible together without scrolling between them.

When a teacher saves an archive action for a shared card, the product shall keep it in the learner's deck but remove it from study; when the teacher attempts permanent deletion, the product shall refuse the request and keep the card.

When the teacher editor offers Archive without permanent deletion, the product shall explain that archiving retains the card and history but removes it from study, and shall omit guidance for an unavailable Delete action.

The product shall show the teacher each shared deck with the student's name, the cards not yet checked, and the cards asked about.

When the teacher opens a card list, the product shall explain Checked, To check, and Asked to check and shall identify explicit learner requests as the cards listed first.

When the teacher scrolls the card list, the product shall keep its introductory instructions and list actions visible while the cards scroll.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a learner who shared deck A, and not deck B, with a teacher, when the teacher opens Teaching, then deck A is listed with the learner's name and deck B is not; a request for deck B or its cards by the teacher's account answers `404`.

### AC-02

Given a card in an unshared deck, when the learner asks a teacher about it, then the request is refused with `409`; given a shared deck, the card is marked and appears first in the teacher's list.

### AC-03

Given a shared card, when the teacher edits its sentence, then the learner's stored card stays unchanged until Save changes is selected; after saving, the learner sees the teacher's sentence in study.

### AC-04

Given a signed-in account with no shared decks, when the user opens Teaching, then the page is available, shows the account's sharing email, and explains that there are no shared decks yet.

### AC-05

Given a shared deck on desktop, when the teacher opens its table, then every row shows its sentence, translation, and approval control without opening the card; opening the card reveals the additional details.

### AC-06

Given a card generated with learner-authored instructions, when the teacher opens it, then those instructions cannot be edited; submitting an attempted teacher change is refused, while corrective feedback can be supplied for regeneration without changing the learner's deck instructions.

### AC-07

Given a shared active card, when the teacher archives it and selects Save changes, then the learner can still find the archived card and it is excluded from study; when the teacher attempts permanent deletion, the request is refused and the learner can still inspect or permanently delete the card.

### AC-08

Given a shared card, when the teacher selects the regeneration action and generation succeeds, then the latest sentence and translation appear below that action in the same editor; on desktop the preview and rewrite actions are visible together without scrolling, and on a phone they remain reachable without returning to the card list or scrolling sideways. The learner's stored card changes only after Save changes is selected.

### AC-09

Given a German card with an English translation, when the teacher opens its details, then separate playback controls are available for all four card elements in their respective languages, while the summary review table contains no pronunciation controls.

### AC-10

Given shared cards with long words and English sentence translations, when the teacher opens the list at a 390-CSS-pixel phone width, then each summary shows its sentence and translation, long content wraps, and the review controls do not cover the text.

### AC-11

Given a card generated from a source sentence containing its selected word, when the teacher opens the editor, then the original sentence appears read-only above the generated sentence, the selected word is highlighted in that context, and the word-editing fields follow the generated sentence and translation.

### AC-12

Given a shared card with a missing article, two saved reviews, and memberships in decks A and B, when the teacher corrects its word or meaning and selects Save changes, then the learner sees the correction on the same card with the same identifier, original source context, memberships, review history, and due date; learner-authored generation instructions remain unchanged.

### AC-13

Given the teacher opens regeneration controls, when they inspect the section, then its heading identifies card regeneration and its help explains corrective feedback, existing generation instructions, and regeneration of both the sentence and its translation.

### AC-14

Given checked cards, unchecked cards without a request, and cards explicitly requested for review, when the teacher opens the list, then a legend or introductory instruction explains each corresponding label and states that requested cards appear first.

### AC-15

Given the teacher list contains more cards than fit on screen, when the teacher scrolls through its cards, then the list instructions and list actions remain visible while the cards move.

### AC-16

Given the teacher editor offers Archive and no Delete action, when its help is displayed, then it explains retention of the card and history and exclusion from study, without explaining a permanent-delete action that is unavailable in that view.
