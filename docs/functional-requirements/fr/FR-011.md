## Behavior

When a learner shares a deck with the email of a teacher who has an account, the product shall show that deck and all its cards to that teacher, and nothing else of the learner's.

When a learner asks the teacher about a card, the product shall accept the request only if the card's deck is shared, and shall list the cards asked about first on the teacher's page.

When a signed-in user opens Teaching, the product shall show the account email used for receiving deck shares and shall make Teaching available even if no deck has been shared with that user.

When the teacher reviews a shared deck on desktop, the product shall show the sentence, translation, and approval control in each table row and shall let the teacher open the card for more information.

When a teacher opens a card's details, the product shall provide separate pronunciation controls there for the word, meaning, sentence, and translation, rather than adding them to the summary table.

When the teacher edits a shared card or its review status, the product shall keep the change pending until the teacher explicitly saves it, and the learner shall study the saved correction.

When the teacher requests regeneration, the product shall accept corrective feedback separately from the learner-authored generation instructions and shall not let the teacher modify those instructions.

When regeneration returns a result, the product shall display the new sentence and translation beside the rewrite actions so the teacher can inspect and retry it without searching another panel; on desktop the preview and rewrite actions shall be visible together without scrolling between them.

When a teacher saves an archive action for a shared card, the product shall keep it in the learner's deck but remove it from study; when the teacher attempts permanent deletion, the product shall refuse the request and keep the card.

The product shall show the teacher each shared deck with the student's name, the cards not yet checked, and the cards asked about.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a learner who shared deck A, and not deck B, with a teacher, when the teacher opens Teaching, then deck A is listed with the learner's name and deck B is not; a request for deck B or its cards by the teacher's account answers `404`.

### AC-02

Given a card in an unshared deck, when the learner asks a teacher about it, then the request is refused with `409`; given a shared deck, the card is marked and appears first in the teacher's list.

### AC-03

Given a shared card, when the teacher edits its sentence, then the learner's stored card stays unchanged until Save changes is selected; after saving, the learner sees the teacher's sentence in study.

### AC-04

Given a signed-in account with no shared decks, when the user opens Teaching, then the page is available, shows the account's sharing email, and explains that there are no shared decks yet.

### AC-05

Given a shared deck on desktop, when the teacher opens its table, then every row shows its sentence, translation, and approval control without opening the card; opening the card reveals the additional details.

### AC-06

Given a card generated with learner-authored instructions, when the teacher opens it, then those instructions cannot be edited; submitting an attempted teacher change is refused, while corrective feedback can be supplied for regeneration without changing the learner's deck instructions.

### AC-07

Given a shared active card, when the teacher archives it and selects Save changes, then the learner can still find the archived card and it is excluded from study; when the teacher attempts permanent deletion, the request is refused and the learner can still inspect or permanently delete the card.

### AC-08

Given a shared card on desktop, when the teacher selects Rewrite now and generation succeeds, then the new sentence and translation appear in the editor with the rewrite actions visible without scrolling to another panel; on a phone the preview and controls remain reachable in the same editor without returning to the card list or scrolling sideways. The learner's stored card changes only after Save changes is selected.

### AC-09

Given a German card with an English translation, when the teacher opens its details, then separate playback controls are available for all four card elements in their respective languages, while the summary review table contains no pronunciation controls.
