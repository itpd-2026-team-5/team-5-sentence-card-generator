# FR-010: Read A Sentence Aloud

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-08`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/41), [`DEC-010`](../../decisions.md#dec-010), [`DEC-039`](../../decisions.md#dec-039)
- **Containers:** `frontend`
- **Changed:**
  - Each part of a card can be heard on its own, in study and when teaching, per the Customer in the Week 4 meeting; its `DEC-nnn` is recorded with that meeting's report.
  - 2026-10-31, per [DEC-088](../../decisions.md#dec-088) and [DEC-089](../../decisions.md#dec-089): preserve the global sound default between sessions, expose its control near the top of the interface, and limit an in-study override to its current session. AC-02 is updated in place to check that session boundary.

## Behavior

When a card is shown in study, the product shall read its sentence aloud in the deck's language, unless the learner switched reading aloud off.

When the learner or a teacher presses the speaker beside the word, its meaning, the sentence, or the translation, the product shall read that part aloud in its own language.

When the learner presses the speaker button for a sentence, the product shall read the sentence aloud again.

When the learner changes the global automatic-reading setting, the product shall preserve that default between study sessions in the same browser and shall make its control available near the top of the interface.

When a learner changes automatic reading during study, the product shall apply that override only to the current session and shall leave the global default unchanged.

When the learner starts a new session, the product shall initialize automatic reading from the global default.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given reading aloud switched on and a German card, when the card is shown, then the browser speaks the sentence with a German voice.

### AC-02

Given a global default of reading aloud on, when the learner switches automatic reading off during a study session, then later cards in that session are not read automatically; after ending it and starting a new session, automatic reading is on again.

### AC-03

Given a German card translated into English, when the learner presses the speaker beside the translation, then the browser speaks the translation with an English voice.

### AC-04

Given the learner switches the global automatic-reading default off, when they start and end two study sessions in the same browser, then both sessions start with automatic reading off, including after reloading the application.

### AC-05

Given a global default of reading aloud off, when the learner enables it inside one session and then starts another session, then the new session starts with automatic reading off and the global control still shows off.

### AC-06

Given Today or a study view at desktop or phone width, when the learner looks near the top of the interface, then the global sound control is visible and can be changed without opening Settings; the in-study override remains separately available.
