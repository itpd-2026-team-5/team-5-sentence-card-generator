# FR-010: Read A Sentence Aloud

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-08`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/41), [`DEC-010`](../../decisions.md#dec-010), [`DEC-039`](../../decisions.md#dec-039)
- **Containers:** `frontend`

## Behavior

When a card is shown in study, the product shall read its sentence aloud in the deck's language, unless the learner switched reading aloud off.

When the learner presses the speaker button, the product shall read the sentence aloud again, and the switch the learner sets shall stay set the next time they study.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given reading aloud switched on and a German card, when the card is shown, then the browser speaks the sentence with a German voice.

### AC-02

Given the learner switches reading aloud off in study and reloads the page, when the next card is shown, then nothing is spoken until they press the speaker button.
