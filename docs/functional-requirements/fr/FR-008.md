## Behavior

When a learner opens Today, the product shall show each deck with its new cards and its cards to review, and shall preselect the decks the learner chose last time.

When the learner starts a session, the product shall show only active cards from the chosen decks.

During a session, the product shall show how many cards are new, how many are reviews from earlier days, and how many are back again today.

When the learner moves a deck up or down on Today, the product shall keep that order and shall offer the chosen decks' new cards in it.

When exactly one deck is selected on Today, the product shall label the English study action Study one deck rather than include the deck's title.

When the learner drags several selected decks to a new position on Today, the product shall move them together, preserve their relative order and selection, and save the resulting deck order.

When deck order changes, the product shall keep each card's identifier, review history, and next due date unchanged.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a learner who chose decks A and B and reloads the page, when Today opens, then A and B are ticked and C is not, also in another browser.

### AC-02

Given decks A and B ticked, when the learner studies, then no card from deck C and no archived or flagged card is shown.

### AC-03

Given a session with 2 new cards and 1 due review, when the learner rates the first new card `Again`, then the counts read 1 new, 1 review, and 1 again.

### AC-04

Given decks A and B, both ticked, with A above B, when the learner moves B to the top and reloads Today, then B is listed first.

### AC-05

Given exactly one selected deck whose title is longer than 100 characters, when Today is displayed in English, then the study action reads Study one deck and does not contain that title.

### AC-06

Given decks A, B, C, D with B and C selected, when the learner drags B and C before A and reloads Today, then the order is B, C, A, D, B and C remain selected, and their new cards are offered in that order.

### AC-07

Given a reviewed card with a saved identifier and next due date, when the learner reorders its deck, then the card's identifier, review history, and next due date are unchanged.
