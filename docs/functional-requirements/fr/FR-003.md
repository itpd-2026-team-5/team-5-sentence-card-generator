# FR-003: Generate A Card For Each Marked Word Into A Deck

- **Traces to:** [`US-03`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/32), [`US-05`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/38), [`US-06`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/39), [`DEC-003`](../../decisions.md#dec-003), [`DEC-007`](../../decisions.md#dec-007), [`DEC-013`](../../decisions.md#dec-013), [`DEC-015`](../../decisions.md#dec-015), [`DEC-024`](../../decisions.md#dec-024), [`DEC-025`](../../decisions.md#dec-025), [`DEC-026`](../../decisions.md#dec-026), [`DEC-048`](../../decisions.md#dec-048), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-002`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-002.md), [`ADR-003`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-003.md)

## Behavior

When a learner generates cards for a text into a deck they choose, the product shall create one card in that deck for each word marked to learn that the deck has no card for yet.

The product shall write each card's sentence in the deck's language, at the deck's level or else the learner's level in that language, following the deck's instructions, in the meaning the word has in its source sentence, and with words the learner knows.

Each card shall carry the new sentence, its translation into the deck's known language, the word as it appears in the sentence, the word's translation in that meaning, the source sentence, and the source text's title.

When a generated sentence fails a check, being without the word, outside the learner's word or character bounds, or using a word the learner chose to ignore, the product shall ask for it again with the reason, up to three attempts in all, and shall mark a card that still fails as flagged rather than ready to study.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a German deck at A2, with `Bank` marked in `Ich sitze auf der Bank im Park.`, when cards are generated, then the deck gains one card whose sentence contains `Bank`, whose word translation is `bench`, and whose sentence is not the source sentence.

### AC-02

Given a deck whose level is `B1` and a learner whose German level is `A2`, when cards are generated, then the request to the LLM asks for `B1`; given a deck with no level, it asks for `A2`.

### AC-03

Given a learner whose bounds are 25 to 90 characters, and a fake LLM that first answers with a 120-character sentence and then with a 60-character one, when cards are generated, then the card holds the 60-character sentence and is active.

### AC-04

Given a fake LLM whose every answer is 120 characters long, when cards are generated, then the LLM is asked three times and the card is saved as flagged and does not appear in study.
