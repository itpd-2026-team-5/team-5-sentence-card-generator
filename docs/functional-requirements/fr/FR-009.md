# FR-009: Schedule Each Review With FSRS

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-10`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/36), [`CON-05`](../../product-vision.md#con-05), [`DEC-005`](../../decisions.md#dec-005), [`DEC-041`](../../decisions.md#dec-041), [`ADR-001`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-001.md), [`ADR-005`](../../architecture/adr/ADR-005.md)
- **Containers:** `backend`
- **Changed:**
  - 2026-11-08, per [DEC-121](../../decisions.md#dec-121): let the learner edit each deck's daily new-card and review limits; changing those limits controls admission to study without rewriting a card's FSRS history or due date.
  - 2026-10-31, per [DEC-094](../../decisions.md#dec-094): the review keeps the existing scheduling behavior; it does not select a manual-card-order algorithm or a tie-breaking rule. The future ordering constraint in [DEC-093](../../decisions.md#dec-093) does not change a card's identifier or review history.

## Behavior

When a learner rates a card `Again`, `Hard`, `Good`, or `Easy`, the product shall schedule its next review with the FSRS algorithm from the card's review history.

Before the learner rates a card, the product shall show the interval each rating would give.

In one day, the product shall start no more new cards from a deck than the deck's new-card limit, and shall show no more reviews from it than the deck's review limit, while cards relearned that day come back without a limit.

When a learner edits a deck, the product shall let them save separate daily new-card and review limits for that deck and shall use those limits when building its study queue.

When the learner changes a deck's daily limits, the product shall keep the deck's existing card review histories and due dates unchanged.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a new card, when the learner rates it `Easy`, then its next review is days away, and when another new card is rated `Again`, then it is due again within 10 minutes.

### AC-02

Given a card on the study screen, when the answer is shown, then each of the four rating buttons shows an interval, and rating the card gives the interval its button showed.

### AC-03

Given a deck with a limit of 2 new cards a day and 5 new cards, when the learner starts 2 of them today, then study offers no third new card from that deck until the next day.

### AC-04

Given the learner owns decks A and B and has studied no cards today, when they save A's limits as 2 new cards and 3 due reviews per day and reload the editor, then those values persist, B's limits are unchanged, and A's queue admits at most 2 new cards and 3 due reviews; same-day relearning remains exempt under the existing rule.

### AC-05

Given a reviewed card with a saved history and due date, when the learner changes its deck's daily limits, then that card's history and due date are unchanged.
