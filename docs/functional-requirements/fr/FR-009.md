# FR-009: Schedule Each Review With FSRS

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-10`](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/issues/36), [`CON-05`](../../product-vision.md#con-05), [`DEC-005`](../../decisions.md#dec-005), [`DEC-041`](../../decisions.md#dec-041)
- **Containers:** `backend`

## Behavior

When a learner rates a card `Again`, `Hard`, `Good`, or `Easy`, the product shall schedule its next review with the FSRS algorithm from the card's review history.

Before the learner rates a card, the product shall show the interval each rating would give.

In one day, the product shall start no more new cards from a deck than the deck's new-card limit, and shall show no more reviews from it than the deck's review limit, while cards relearned that day come back without a limit.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a new card, when the learner rates it `Easy`, then its next review is days away, and when another new card is rated `Again`, then it is due again within 10 minutes.

### AC-02

Given a card on the study screen, when the answer is shown, then each of the four rating buttons shows an interval, and rating the card gives the interval its button showed.

### AC-03

Given a deck with a limit of 2 new cards a day and 5 new cards, when the learner starts 2 of them today, then study offers no third new card from that deck until the next day.
