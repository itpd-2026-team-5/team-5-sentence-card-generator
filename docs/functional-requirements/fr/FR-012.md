# FR-012: Configure Interface And Learning Preferences

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [DEC-025](../../decisions.md#dec-025), [DEC-026](../../decisions.md#dec-026), [DEC-052](../../decisions.md#dec-052), [DEC-083](../../decisions.md#dec-083), [DEC-085](../../decisions.md#dec-085), [DEC-086](../../decisions.md#dec-086), [DEC-090](../../decisions.md#dec-090)
- **Containers:** `frontend`, `backend`

## Behavior

When a learner chooses an interface language, the product shall display interface text in that language while keeping each interface-language option written in its own language.

When the learner sees learning or translation language names elsewhere in the interface, the product shall display those names in the selected interface language.

When a learner adds or changes a learning language, the product shall let them select that language and its proficiency level together in the per-language level controls.

When the learner opens Settings, the product shall omit the separate Languages for new decks card; destination language-pair selection is provided by [FR-003](FR-003.md) during generation, and a deck can still override its level.

When the learner edits sentence-length bounds, the product shall align the range separator vertically with the minimum and maximum inputs on desktop and phone layouts.

Provider-token counts may be omitted from the user-facing Settings statistics under [DEC-090](../../decisions.md#dec-090); that permission does not require removing internal usage collection or the existing card-quality statistics.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given the interface switched to German, when the learner opens its language picker, then the options remain English, Deutsch, and Русский; learning-language names elsewhere use the German interface labels.

### AC-02

Given German at level A2, when the learner adds Russian and chooses B1 in the per-language proficiency controls, then both language-level pairs are visible and saving does not change German's A2 level.

### AC-03

Given the learner opens Settings, then there is no separate Languages for new decks card; learning languages can be selected with their levels, while generation's language pair can be chosen through [FR-003 AC-06](FR-003.md#ac-06).

### AC-04

Given sentence-length bounds displayed on desktop and at a 390-CSS-pixel phone width, when the learner edits their values, then the separator is vertically centered with the two inputs rather than aligned to their bottom edges.
