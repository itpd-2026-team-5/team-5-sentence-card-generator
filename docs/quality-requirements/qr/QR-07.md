# QR-07: Preserve Data Across Service Restarts

Committed texts, word selections, decks, cards, sharing permissions, and study progress survive a service shutdown and restart without database corruption.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Recoverability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

The operator of the deployed service.

### Stimulus

The operator stops and restarts the application and database services, as required by [DEC-069](../../decisions.md#dec-069).

### Environment

An existing Linux deployment with persisted application data and no deliberate deletion of its database volume.
The checks include a restart after a completed write and a stop while a write is in progress.

### Artifact

The backend's database transactions, persistent database storage, and the application state loaded after restart.

### Response

The product shall recover committed state without losing or partially applying records.
An interrupted transaction shall be committed completely or rolled back completely, and the accounts shall be able to continue their workflows after restart.

### Response Measure

Across three stop-and-start cycles, every previously committed text, selection, deck, card, permission, and review record retains its identifier and values.
The database integrity check passes, no partial card or sharing record is present, and a learner can resume a previously scheduled review.
For an interrupted write, either the complete new state or the preceding committed state is present.
