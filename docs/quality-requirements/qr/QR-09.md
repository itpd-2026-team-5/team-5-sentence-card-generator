# QR-09: Warn About Problematic Generation Batches

The learner sees a warning when 30% or more of the cards in a generation batch require regeneration.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Reliability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Fault Tolerance
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

The LLM provider responding to one learner's generation batch.

### Stimulus

The batch contains enough invalid or unusable first responses to reach the initial 30% warning threshold from [DEC-072](../../decisions.md#dec-072).

### Environment

One generation batch, with the provider and model used for that batch; aggregate statistics from other models or users are excluded.
For the verification examples, each distinct requested card is counted once, and a card needing multiple retries contributes one problematic card.
This counting convention is the team's proposed operational definition, to be confirmed through the [batch-counting follow-up](../../../reports/week-04/meeting-report.md#open-questions); it is not an additional Customer verdict.

### Artifact

The generation validation results and the user-visible batch feedback, tracked separately from the basic generation workflow per [DEC-076](../../decisions.md#dec-076).

### Response

The product shall warn the learner that the current batch has a high regeneration rate and suggest checking the prompt, instructions, or model.
The warning shall identify the current batch and shall not claim that unrelated global statistics establish model quality.
A rejected or expired key shall instead receive the immediate error feedback specified in [QR-05](QR-05.md).

### Response Measure

For ten distinct requested cards, batches with zero, one, or two problematic cards do not trigger this warning, while three or more do.
Repeated retries for one card do not inflate the number of distinct problematic cards in these checks.
A previous batch's results do not change the current batch's warning, and an invalid key is reported regardless of the percentage.
The 80% first-generation suitability target in [QR-03](QR-03.md) remains a separate quality measure.
