# QR-04: Generate A Text's Cards Within A Minute

Cards for five marked words appear within 60 seconds of the learner pressing generate.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Performance Efficiency
- **Sub-characteristic:** Time Behaviour
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Medium

## Scenario

### Source

A learner in the reader, on a laptop or a phone.

### Stimulus

The learner generates cards for a text with five words marked to learn, into a deck that has none of them.

### Environment

The service's LLM key, the deployed product under the load of one learner, and generated sentences that fail a check and are written again.

### Artifact

The generate action, from the `frontend` through the `backend` to the LLM provider and back.

### Response

The product shall show the new cards, or a message saying why there are none, without the learner reloading the page.

### Response Measure

In at least 9 of 10 runs, the five cards are shown within 60 seconds of the press; no run leaves the learner waiting more than 120 seconds without a card or a message.
