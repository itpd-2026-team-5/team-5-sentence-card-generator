# QR-11: Make Cards From A Text On First Use

A learner who has never used the product finds it easy to turn words from their own text into sentence cards, on first use and without help.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Learnability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

Learnability is what [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) measures, because every participant meets the product for the first time and must work out the text-to-card flow from the interface alone, and the main task's SEQ reads it, because it is the participant's own rating of how easy that first attempt was, taken straight after it.

The measure is set here before the first counted session and is not changed after the data; the team does not coach participants towards it.
The main task's completion and assistance, its time on task, the secondary tasks' SEQ, and the SUS are not read by this requirement; they are reported as results in [`results.md`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md).

## Scenario

### Source

A representative participant who matches [UT-01's participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile): an adult learning Russian who has used a flashcard app but never this product.

### Stimulus

The participant attempts [UT-01's main usability test task](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#main-task-turn-words-from-a-text-into-cards): keep a provided Russian text, choose two words from it, and get study cards for them in their Russian deck.

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university network, with the demo data reset from the seed before the session, during an in-person usability session run per [UT-01's protocol](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#protocol).

### Artifact

The product's text, word-marking, and card-generation flow in the web frontend, as a learner reaches it after sign-in.

### Response

The participant works through the main task and then rates how difficult or easy it was on the [SEQ](../../usability-tests/ut-01/surveys.md#seq-after-each-task), from 1, very difficult, to 7, very easy.

### Response Measure

Across at least three counted participants, the median SEQ for the main task is at least 5 of 7.

## QRT-01

The counted sessions of `UT-01` give a median main-task SEQ of at least 5 of 7.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** the counted usability sessions of [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md), read from its [`results.md`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md).
- **Why Manual:** the measure is each participant's SEQ rating after the main task, which only a person can give in a facilitated session, so no command can produce or judge it, and the test reads no `SLI`.

### Procedure

1. Run `UT-01` per its record: at least three counted sessions with participants who match the [participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile), on the staging deployment with the seed reset before each, following the [protocol](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#protocol) and the [session sheet](../../usability-tests/ut-01/sessions/README.md).
2. After each participant's main task, record their [SEQ](../../usability-tests/ut-01/surveys.md#seq-after-each-task) answer on the observation sheet, whatever the task's outcome; a participant who fails the main task still answers it and still counts.
3. Write the per-participant table and the main task's median SEQ in [`results.md`](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), per the [analysis plan](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#analysis-plan), with `n=<count>`.
   Count only the sessions the record counts; a dry run, a pilot, or a session without the required consent is excluded.
4. Compare the median with the bar, and record the verdict, `Checked: SPRINT-08`, and the `results.md` evidence in the [testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan); the raw answers stay in the private usability-data repository.

**Pass when:** at least three counted participants answered the main task's SEQ, and the median of their answers is at least 5 of 7.
