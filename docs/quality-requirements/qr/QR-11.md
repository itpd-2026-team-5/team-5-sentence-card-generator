# QR-11: Complete The First Card-Making Task Without Help

Representative first-time learners can turn chosen words from a text into study cards without help.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Learnability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

Learnability is the chosen attribute because the main task begins with a learner who has never used the product. The unaided-completion proportion measures whether such learners can discover and use the flow without instruction; task time and ratings remain separate analysis results.

## Scenario

### Source

An adult learning Russian or German, with or without prior flashcard-app experience, who matches the [UT-01 participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile) and has not used this product before.

### Stimulus

The participant attempts [UT-01's main task](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#main-task-turn-words-from-a-text-into-cards): keep the provided text, choose two words, and get study cards for them in the session's chosen-language deck. The profile and task-language changes follow [DEC-143](../../decisions.md#dec-143) and [DEC-144](../../decisions.md#dec-144).

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university network, on the team's laptop, with the seeded learner account reset before each session and working card generation. At least three consented participants matching the profile complete a counted session.

### Artifact

The learner's text, word-selection, card-generation, and deck views.

### Response

The participant reaches the main task's end state within its ten-minute limit without help from the facilitator.

### Response Measure

The measure is `100 * Unaided / n`, where `n` is the number of counted participants and `Unaided` is the number reaching the main task's end state within ten minutes without help under [UT-01's success and help criteria](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#success-criteria). Assisted and Failed outcomes remain in the denominator and do not meet the measure. At least three counted participants are required.

The pass threshold is a single percentage `T`, **pending Customer confirmation** within the 80-90% expectation recorded in [DEC-146](../../decisions.md#dec-146). The former 60% bar was rejected and is not an active criterion. The meeting did not settle whether 80% or 90% should apply.
Before the first counted session, KaramKhaddour must obtain confirmation of `T` and its use of the Unaided measure, record the confirmation source, and put the same exact percentage in this response measure, QRT-01, and the UT-01 analysis plan. Do not select or change the bar after seeing session outcomes. Until then the design is not ready for counted sessions, and the QRT remains Not verified.

Once confirmed, the required Unaided count is `ceil(n * T / 100)`; do not round the observed percentage up. With five counted participants, 80% would require four Unaided outcomes and 90% would require five; these illustrate the calculation and do not select the threshold.

## QRT-01

The UT-01 sessions measure whether first-time learners can complete the main card-making task unaided.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) on the staging deployment, with at least three counted participants.
- **Why Manual:** An observer must judge whether the participant reached the end state and whether any facilitator action counted as help. A command or an `SLI` cannot determine that outcome; this test reads no `SLI`.

### Procedure

1. Before the first counted session, confirm that the exact percentage T and its confirmation source have been recorded consistently in this requirement and the UT-01 analysis plan; stop if the threshold is still pending. Confirm consent, the participant profile, the reset seed, working staging generation, and verified instrumentation as specified in [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md).
2. Run the same main task with at least three counted participants representing the revised profile, including both learning languages and flashcard-app newcomers, using the [session sheet](../../usability-tests/ut-01/sessions/README.md). For each, record `Unaided`, `Assisted`, or `Failed`, the elapsed time, and any help in the private observation sheet; publish only the sanitized session note.
3. In [UT-01 results](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), report the counted sample size, the number of `Unaided` main-task outcomes, and their proportion. Keep the other task measures and limitations in the results as the analysis plan requires.
4. When the pre-session criterion is fixed and the sessions are analyzed, record `Passes` or `Fails`, the check sprint, and a link to the sanitized results in the [testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan). Until the sessions are analyzed, leave the row `Not verified`.

**Pass when:** the exact pre-session threshold T has been confirmed and recorded, at least three eligible participants have completed counted sessions, and `100 * Unaided / n >= T`, equivalently `Unaided >= ceil(n * T / 100)`, for main-task completion within ten minutes without help. An unset threshold or fewer than three counted participants leaves the test `Not verified`. The [testing-plan row](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan) stays `Not verified`, planned for SPRINT-08, until the results are analyzed.
