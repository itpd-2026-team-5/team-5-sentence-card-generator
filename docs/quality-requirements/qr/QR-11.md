# QR-11: Complete The First Card-Making Task Without Help

Representative first-time learners can turn chosen words from a text into study cards without help.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Learnability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium

Learnability is the chosen attribute because the main task begins with a learner who has never used the product. The unaided-completion proportion measures whether such learners can discover and use the flow without instruction; task time and ratings remain separate analysis results.

## Scenario

### Source

An adult learning Russian who matches the [UT-01 participant profile](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#participant-profile) and has not used this product before.

### Stimulus

The participant attempts [UT-01's main task](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#main-task-turn-words-from-a-text-into-cards): keep the provided text, choose two words, and get study cards for them in the Russian deck.

### Environment

The staging deployment on the university network, on the team's laptop, with the seeded learner account reset before each session and working card generation. At least three consented participants matching the profile complete a counted session.

### Artifact

The learner's text, word-selection, card-generation, and deck views.

### Response

The participant reaches the main task's end state within its ten-minute limit without help from the facilitator.

### Response Measure

At least 60% of counted participants, with at least three counted participants, have a main-task outcome of `Unaided` under [UT-01's success and help criteria](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md#success-criteria). The observer records each outcome before analysis. The proportion is the number of `Unaided` main-task outcomes divided by the number of counted participants; `Assisted` and `Failed` do not meet the measure. This bar is set before any counted session.

## QRT-01

The UT-01 sessions measure whether first-time learners can complete the main card-making task unaided.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md) on the staging deployment, with at least three counted participants.
- **Why Manual:** An observer must judge whether the participant reached the end state and whether any facilitator action counted as help. A command or an `SLI` cannot determine that outcome; this test reads no `SLI`.

### Procedure

1. Before the first counted session, confirm consent, the participant profile, the reset seed, working staging generation, and verified instrumentation as specified in [UT-01](../../usability-tests/ut-01/README.md).
2. Run the same main task with at least three counted participants using the [session sheet](../../usability-tests/ut-01/sessions/README.md). For each, record `Unaided`, `Assisted`, or `Failed`, the elapsed time, and any help in the private observation sheet; publish only the sanitized session note.
3. In [UT-01 results](../../usability-tests/ut-01/results.md), report the counted sample size, the number of `Unaided` main-task outcomes, and their proportion. Keep the other task measures and limitations in the results as the analysis plan requires.
4. Record `Passes` or `Fails`, the check sprint, and a link to the sanitized results in the [testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan). Until the sessions are analyzed, leave the row `Not verified`.

**Pass when:** at least three participants have completed counted sessions and at least 60% of them completed the main task `Unaided` within ten minutes, without help. A smaller sample leaves the test `Not verified`.
