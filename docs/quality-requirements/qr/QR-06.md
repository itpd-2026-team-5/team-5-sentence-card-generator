# QR-06: Use Learner And Teacher Workflows On A Phone

A learner or teacher completes their main card workflow on a phone without zooming or scrolling sideways.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium
- **Changed:** 2026-10-24, per [DEC-059](../../decisions.md#dec-059) and [DEC-071](../../decisions.md#dec-071): cover the teacher workflow and long deck lists as well as study, with higher importance and risk because the current tables need rework.

## Scenario

### Source

A learner or teacher on a phone, including the Customer's mobile study use described in the [Week 2 prototype record](../../../reports/week-02/prototypes.md) and the teacher-table review behind [DEC-071](../../decisions.md#dec-071).

### Stimulus

The learner selects decks and studies ten cards, or the teacher finds a requested card, reads its note, edits it, and saves the review.

### Environment

Chrome or Firefox on a phone with a screen 360 to 430 CSS pixels wide, per [`CON-02`](../../product-vision.md#con-02), with no keyboard.

### Artifact

The frontend's primary text, settings, deck, study, and teaching views, including card editors and their actions.

### Response

The product shall fit its primary views to the phone's width and keep study and teacher-review actions reachable by a tap.
Study controls shall remain accessible without scrolling through the full deck list; an individual-card detail view may replace a wide table.

### Response Measure

At 360 and 390 CSS pixels wide, none of the primary views scrolls sideways; study and teacher-review buttons are at least 40 by 40 CSS pixels.
A ten-card study session and a requested-card teacher review, including saving the changes, are completed with taps only and without zooming.
With 100 selectable decks, the Study action is reachable without scrolling to the end of the list.
