# QR-06: Use Learner And Teacher Workflows On A Phone

A learner or teacher completes their main card workflow on a phone without zooming or scrolling sideways.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** Medium
- **Changed:**
  - 2026-10-24, per [DEC-059](../../decisions.md#dec-059) and [DEC-071](../../decisions.md#dec-071): cover the teacher workflow and long deck lists as well as study, with higher importance and risk because the current tables need rework.
  - 2026-10-31, per [DEC-087](../../decisions.md#dec-087), [DEC-099](../../decisions.md#dec-099), and [DEC-100](../../decisions.md#dec-100): require usable navigation and a reachable regeneration preview as well as a layout that fits the screen; the review exposed missing mobile navigation, so general responsive-layout feedback is not a passing test.

## Scenario

### Source

A learner or teacher on a phone, including the Customer's mobile study use described in the [Week 2 prototype record](../../../reports/week-02/prototypes.md) and the teacher-table review behind [DEC-071](../../decisions.md#dec-071).

### Stimulus

The learner selects decks and studies ten cards, or the teacher finds a requested card, reads its note, edits or regenerates its sentence, inspects the result, and saves the review.

### Environment

Chrome or Firefox on a phone with a screen 360 to 430 CSS pixels wide, per [`CON-02`](../../product-vision.md#con-02), with no keyboard.

### Artifact

The frontend's primary text, settings, deck, study, and teaching views, including card editors and their actions.

### Response

The product shall fit its primary views to the phone's width and keep study and teacher-review actions reachable by a tap.
Study controls shall remain accessible without scrolling through the full deck list; an individual-card detail view may replace a wide table.
Navigation between the primary sections shall remain reachable from each view without entering a URL or using the browser's Back button.
After teacher regeneration, the preview and retry controls shall remain accessible in the same editor without returning to the card list.

### Response Measure

At 360 and 390 CSS pixels wide, none of the primary views scrolls sideways; study and teacher-review buttons are at least 40 by 40 CSS pixels.
A ten-card study session and a requested-card teacher review, including saving the changes, are completed with taps only and without zooming.
With 100 selectable decks, the Study action is reachable without scrolling to the end of the list.
At both widths, the learner and teacher can navigate from Settings to Today, decks, and Teaching using the application's navigation, and the teacher can inspect a regenerated sentence and retry it without sideways scrolling or leaving the editor.

## QRT-01

The learner's and the teacher's primary views fit a 360 and a 390 CSS-pixel-wide phone screen, with their action buttons large enough to tap.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** scripted
- **Runs at:** a development machine with the product running locally: `node scripts/check-phone-layout.mjs http://localhost:5173`, a headless browser check.

### Procedure

1. Start the product locally with the demo data, per [Set up and run](../../setup.md), and add decks until the demo learner has 100.
2. Run `node scripts/check-phone-layout.mjs http://localhost:5173`.
   At 360 and 390 CSS pixels wide, it signs in as the demo learner and the demo teacher, opens the texts, settings, decks, study, and teaching views, and a card editor.
   It uses the application's navigation to move between the primary sections and checks the teacher's regeneration preview with a controlled successful generation response.
3. For each view and width, it prints whether the page is wider than the screen, the smallest study and teacher-review button in CSS pixels, and whether the Study action is on screen before the end of the deck list.
   It also prints whether section navigation works and the regenerated preview and retry controls are reachable in the same editor.

**Pass when:** at both widths, no view is wider than the screen, no study or teacher-review button is smaller than 40 by 40 CSS pixels, the Study action is on screen, navigation works between the primary sections, and the regenerated preview and retry controls are reachable in the same editor.

**Implementation status:** the scripted procedure is planned; `scripts/check-phone-layout.mjs` is not yet present in the repository.
It must also check the navigation and regeneration-preview measures above before a passing result is recorded in the [testing plan](../../project-plan.md#quality-requirement-testing-plan).
