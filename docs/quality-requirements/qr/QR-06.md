# QR-06: Study A Whole Session On A Phone

A learner completes a study session on a phone with one thumb, without zooming or scrolling sideways.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Interaction Capability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Operability
- **Business importance:** Medium
- **Technical risk:** Low

## Scenario

### Source

A learner on a phone, on the go, as most of the Customer's users study (the validation meeting on 2026-10-09, in the [Week 2 prototype record](https://github.com/itpd-2026-team-5/team-5-sentence-card-generator/blob/main/reports/week-02/prototypes.md)).

### Stimulus

The learner opens Today, chooses decks, and studies until no card is due.

### Environment

Chrome or Firefox on a phone with a screen 360 to 430 CSS pixels wide, per [`CON-02`](../../product-vision.md#con-02), with no keyboard.

### Artifact

Today and the study screen in the `frontend`.

### Response

The product shall fit each screen to the phone's width and shall keep every action of a session, from revealing the answer to rating and marking a card, reachable by a tap.

### Response Measure

At 360 and 390 CSS pixels wide, no study screen scrolls sideways, every button of a session is at least 40 by 40 CSS pixels, and a session of 10 cards is completed with taps only.
