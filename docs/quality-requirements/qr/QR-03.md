# QR-03: Write A Sentence The Learner Can Keep

At least 80% of the cards generated for a learner's marked words can be kept without editing or rewriting, in each supported language.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Functional Suitability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Functional Correctness
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** High
- **Changed:** 2026-10-24, per [DEC-072](../../decisions.md#dec-072): distinguish the per-batch regeneration warning from the unchanged 80% first-generation quality target.

## Scenario

### Source

A learner who marked words in a text they chose.

### Stimulus

The learner generates cards for the marked words into a deck.

### Environment

The service's LLM, the learner's level for the deck's language, the deck's instructions, and the learner's sentence bounds, with the learner's known words taken from their earlier texts.

### Artifact

The card generation in the `backend`, and the cards it saves.

### Response

The product shall save, for each marked word, a card whose sentence differs from the source sentence, uses the word in its source meaning, fits the bounds and the level, and carries a correct translation of the sentence and of the word.

### Response Measure

Out of 30 consecutive first-generated cards for distinct words in each of Russian, English, and German, at least 24 pass the reviewer and learner checks of [`TOS-02`](../../threshold-of-success.md#tos-02); a failed or missing card counts as a failure.

The 30% operational warning in [QR-09](QR-09.md) concerns a single batch and does not lower this first-generation quality target or establish that a model has passed it.

## QRT-01

At least 24 of 30 first-generated cards for distinct words can be kept without editing, in each of Russian, English, and German.

- **Status:** Active
- **Level:** manual
- **Runs at:** a reviewer and a learner reading the cards generated on the staging deployment, per the checks of [`TOS-02`](../../threshold-of-success.md#tos-02).
- **Why Manual:** whether a sentence fits the word's meaning, the learner's level, and a correct translation is the reviewer's and the learner's judgement, which no command can assert.

### Procedure

1. On staging, with the service's LLM configured, add one text per language with at least 30 distinct words marked to learn, and generate them into a deck at the learner's level.
2. For each of the first 30 cards per language, the reviewer and the learner record whether it can be kept without editing or rewriting, counting a failed or missing card as not kept.
3. Count the kept cards per language.

**Pass when:** the count is at least 24 in each of the three languages.
