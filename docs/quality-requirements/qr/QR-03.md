# QR-03: Write A Sentence The Learner Can Keep

At least 80% of the cards generated for a learner's marked words can be kept without editing or rewriting, in each supported language.

- **Status:** Active
- **Characteristic:** Functional Suitability
- **Sub-characteristic:** Functional Correctness
- **Business importance:** High
- **Technical risk:** High

## Scenario

### Source

A learner who marked words in a text they chose.

### Stimulus

The learner generates cards for the marked words into a deck.

### Environment

The service's LLM, the learner's level for the deck's language, the deck's instructions, and the learner's sentence bounds, with the learner's known words taken from their earlier texts.

### Artifact

The card generation in the `backend`, and the cards it saves.

### Response

The product shall save, for each marked word, a card whose sentence differs from the source sentence, uses the word in its source meaning, fits the bounds and the level, and carries a correct translation of the sentence and of the word.

### Response Measure

Out of 30 consecutive first-generated cards for distinct words in each of Russian, English, and German, at least 24 pass the reviewer and learner checks of [`TOS-02`](../../threshold-of-success.md#tos-02); a failed or missing card counts as a failure.
