# ADR-004: Share A Deck Through An Explicit Share Record

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Created:** 2026-10-24
- **Updated:** 2026-10-24
- **Containers:** `backend`, `database`
- **Addresses:** [`FR-011`](../../functional-requirements/fr/FR-011.md), [`QR-01`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md)
- **Decision:** [`DEC-036`](../../decisions.md#dec-036), [`DEC-044`](../../decisions.md#dec-044)

## Context

A learner shares a deck with a teacher, who reviews and corrects all its cards ([`DEC-044`](../../decisions.md#dec-044)) and is the only one who can be asked about a card ([`DEC-036`](../../decisions.md#dec-036)).
The teacher must see nothing else of the learner's, neither other decks nor texts nor the key, which [`TOS-03`](../../threshold-of-success.md#tos-03) checks with direct requests.

## Considered Options

- **A share row per deck and teacher, checked on every request.**
  The backend reaches a card for a teacher only through a share of one of the card's decks, and answers `404` for everything else.
- **A teacher role on the account that sees all of a learner's decks.**
  Simpler, but the learner could not keep a deck private, which the Customer requires.
- **A share link that anyone holding it can open.**
  No teacher account needed, but anyone who gets the link could read and change the deck.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen option: a share row per deck and teacher account, so access is granted one deck at a time and checked in the backend on every request.
A request for anything not reachable through a share answers `404`, so the teacher cannot tell an unshared deck from one that does not exist.

## Consequences

- **Security:** the access rule is in one function that every teacher request passes, which [`QR-01`](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-01.md) checks with direct requests.
- **Cost:** the teacher needs an account before the learner can share, and the learner has to type the teacher's email.
