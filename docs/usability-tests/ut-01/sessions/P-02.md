# P-02

- **Relation to the target user:** an adult student learning German at A1, for about a year.
- **Familiarity:** None.
- **Learning language:** German
- **Interview language:** English
- **Device:** the participant's own computer and browser, in a remote session on 2026-11-28, after the Customer review, not the team's laptop; see [the results' limitations](../results.md#limitations).
- **Counted:** Yes.

Main task: `Unaided`, 127 seconds. The participant was unsure where to start on the home page, was told to explore, found **Texts**, saved the text, marked two words, and generated two cards.
Secondary task 1: `Unaided`, 154 seconds. The participant opened a deck and studied three cards; they did not know at first whether the ratings judge the word or the sentence, and did not connect decks with cards.
Secondary task 2: `Assisted`, 78 seconds. The participant thought the cards made in the main task had not been saved and began to redo them; the facilitator said the cards were saved, and the participant then found them and rewrote a sentence.

- **Help:** secondary task 2, the facilitator said the generated cards were saved.
- **SEQ:** main 5, secondary 1 6, secondary 2 3.
- **SUS:** 75.0.
- **Themes:** no cue on the home page about where to start; could not tell where the chosen words' cards were kept; asked for a pop-up saying where new cards are saved and how to reach them; adding a text, choosing words, and rewriting felt intuitive once found.
- **Quote:** "If it can tell me where they are saved and how can I go to them." (published with consent)
