# P-04

- **Relation to the target user:** an adult who studies and works, learning German at A1.
- **Familiarity:** has used Anki before; no flashcard app in current use.
- **Learning language:** German
- **Interview language:** English
- **Device:** the participant's own computer and browser, in a remote session on 2026-11-28, not the team's laptop; see [the results' limitations](../results.md#limitations).
- **Counted:** Yes.

Main task: `Unaided`, 94 seconds. The participant asked whether to save the text or the words, was told only the scenario again, found where to save the text, marked two words, and generated two cards.
Secondary task 1: `Unaided`, 113 seconds. The participant went to the decks, opened the German deck, and studied more than three cards.
Secondary task 2: `Unaided`, 29 seconds. The participant opened a card and used **Rewrite the sentence**, reading its label.

- **Help:** None.
- **SEQ:** main 6, secondary 1 6, secondary 2 7.
- **SUS:** 90.0.
- **Themes:** did not know what **Again**, **Hard**, **Good**, and **Easy** mean at first; asked for a short walk-through or an About slide explaining what the app is for; highlighting a word to learn felt easy.
- **Quote:** "It's pretty easy to just point or highlight the word and get to learn it." (published with consent)
