# P-05

- **Relation to the target user:** an adult student learning Russian, at about A2.
- **Familiarity:** RemNote and Anki.
- **Learning language:** Russian
- **Interview language:** English
- **Device:** the participant's own computer and browser, in a remote session on 2026-11-27, not the team's laptop; see [the results' limitations](../results.md#limitations).
- **Counted:** Yes.

Main task: `Unaided`, 295 seconds. The participant found **Add text**, marked two words, generated two cards into the Russian deck, and read the generated sentences and translations without help.
Secondary task 1: `Assisted`, 60 seconds. The participant had already studied the Russian deck when the facilitator moved on to studying inside the main task's time; during that study they asked what **Hard**, **Good**, and **Easy** mean, and the facilitator explained it. When the task itself was read, the session showed "Session complete", and the facilitator counted the earlier study answers.
Secondary task 2: `Unaided`, 46 seconds. The participant opened a card, passed over **Mark to rewrite with others**, and replaced the sentence of another card.

- **Help:** secondary task 1, the facilitator explained that a rating sets how often a card is repeated.
- **SEQ:** main 5, secondary 1 7, secondary 2 7.
- **SUS:** 92.5.
- **Themes:** the study rating buttons' meaning was unclear on first use; opened the seeded German deck first while looking for the Russian one; asked for a first-use tutorial that highlights the controls; adding a text and choosing words felt smooth.
- **Quote:** "Adding the text, choosing the words from the text you have is like a very good UI to look at the word and the translation of it." (published with consent)
