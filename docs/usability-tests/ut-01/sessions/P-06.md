# P-06

- **Relation to the target user:** an adult who studies and works, learning Russian at about A2.
- **Familiarity:** None.
- **Learning language:** Russian
- **Interview language:** English
- **Device:** the participant's own computer and browser, in a remote session on 2026-11-27, not the team's laptop; see [the results' limitations](../results.md#limitations).
- **Counted:** Yes.

Main task: `Unaided`, 173 seconds. The participant asked where to save the text, was told only to explore, found **Texts** and **Add text**, marked two words, and generated two cards; they then looked for the cards under the decks.
Secondary task 1: `Unaided`, 83 seconds. The participant studied the Russian deck and saved answers without help.
Secondary task 2: `Unaided`, 30 seconds. The participant first took **Mark to rewrite with others** for regeneration, read its hover text, and used the other control to get a new sentence.

- **Help:** None.
- **SEQ:** main 7, secondary 1 6, secondary 2 5.
- **SUS:** 85.0.
- **Themes:** the two rewrite icons look alike, and the hover text was needed to tell them apart; asked for a first-use tutorial or short video; the navigation menu made adding a text intuitive.
- **Quote:** "These two symbols, they are a bit confusing." (published with consent)
