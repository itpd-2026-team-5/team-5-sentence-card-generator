# P-07

- **Relation to the target user:** an adult student learning Russian for two months, at about A1.
- **Familiarity:** None; makes their own word lists with a chatbot, and did not know what a study card or a deck is.
- **Learning language:** Russian
- **Interview language:** English
- **Device:** the participant's own computer and browser, in a remote session on 2026-11-27, not the team's laptop; see [the results' limitations](../results.md#limitations).
- **Counted:** Yes.

Main task: `Assisted`, 210 seconds. The participant read the text aloud, asked where to save it, and found **Texts** after the facilitator named it; they then marked two words and generated two cards.
Secondary task 1: `Failed`, 248 seconds. The participant could not find the two new cards among the seeded decks, saw no cards to study, and gave up; the facilitator had pointed them to the Russian deck during the attempt.
Secondary task 2: `Assisted`, 109 seconds. After the facilitator said the cards are kept in a different place from study, the participant found the deck, opened a new card, and regenerated its sentence; the new sentence appeared only after a delay with no progress shown.

- **Help:** main task, the facilitator named **Texts**; secondary task 1, the facilitator pointed to the Russian deck; secondary task 2, the facilitator said the cards are saved in a different place from the study screen.
- **SEQ:** main 6, secondary 1 1, secondary 2 5.
- **SUS:** 50.0.
- **Themes:** could not find the generated cards after generation; the seeded German deck and existing cards mixed with their own; the card list did not look like cards; no progress indicator while regenerating; wanted a short introduction before the tasks.
- **Quote:** "There should be some animation here to show that it's working. Otherwise, I will keep pressing three, four times." (published with consent)
