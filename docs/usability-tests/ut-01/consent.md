# UT-01 Consent Template

This is the unfilled form for one [UT-01 usability session](README.md). The team records consent before the session and keeps the signed copy in the private usability-data repository. The participant receives a copy.

## What I Am Agreeing To

I am at least 18 years old. I am choosing to take part in a study of a language-learning app. I will try three tasks, answer questions before and after them, and may say what I am thinking while I use the app. The session takes about 40 minutes. The team is testing the app, not me. I may skip a question or stop at any time without giving a reason.

The team will record my screen and voice during the session. The observer will also record task outcomes, time on task, assistance, points of difficulty, my survey answers, and notes on what I say. The app's staging analytics records limited task events under a participant code such as `P-01`; it does not put my name or contact details in those events. The team will tell me which screen and voice recording tool and survey or note-taking tool it uses before I sign.

The signed consent, the mapping between my identity and participant code, raw notes, survey answers, filtered analytics export, and working analysis are kept in a **private GitHub repository**. The recording is kept separately in a **team-owned cloud folder**. The team and course instructors can access those private materials to check the study. The public product repository will contain only sanitized session notes, aggregate results, and findings. It will not contain my identity, contact details, raw notes, signed consent, or recording.

The team may publish the sanitized results of the study, including my task outcomes and ratings, without identifying me. My words may appear as an anonymized direct quote **only if I separately agree below**. Declining quotes does not prevent me from taking part.

After transferring the records to their designated private locations, the team deletes working copies left in the recorder, survey, note-taking, or export tools and on the laptop. The private repository, including its history, and the cloud recordings are deleted after the course has been graded. The sanitized public record remains.

I may withdraw during or after the session by contacting the team through the contact method given to me with this form. If I withdraw before the course is graded, the team removes my raw text data from the private repository and my recording from the cloud folder, retires my participant code, and does not count my session. If the session has no recording or either required consent below is withheld, the team does not count it and removes its data.
